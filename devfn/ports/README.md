# @devfn/ports

Machine-local, lock-protected port leases with worktree instance identity, exact/preferred/range/ephemeral allocation, stale reconciliation, listener discovery, Docker mapping discovery, and generated inventory reports. Reports enumerate the complete machine registry, including other projects and profiles, and are not a current-selection view.

Selected proxy routes claim Caddy's listener ports across TCP and UDP. A normal sibling reservation, reconciliation, and garbage collection recover an expired claim only after its service leases are inactive, neither committed nor pending proxy state contains its routes, and direct evidence proves that no listener remains. Successful socket inspection is not enough because a non-dumpable owner (a setcap Caddy on Linux) hides its sockets: every claimed port must refuse loopback connections on 127.0.0.1 and, when available, ::1; an ordinary port must also bind on each loopback family and for UDP (HTTP/3). A verified live Caddy owner may keep its admin listener after its last site is removed, but its live admin configuration must be readable and name no HTTP server listener on a claimed port; an absent, dead, or reused owner requires a free admin listener. Malformed route or owner state and unverified owner identity retain the claim for inspection. A healthy route or bound proxy listener keeps the ports protected while sibling worktrees start and stop.
A port targeted by another instance's committed or pending proxy route stays unavailable to every other instance, even after its lease ended, until a route transition replaces or removes that route; the routed instance itself may lease it again to repair the route. Unreadable proxy route state fails reservation closed. Replacement preflight applies the same abandoned-claim recovery and routed-port rule before checking exact ports. Registry, domain and proxy mutations share a routing lock, so sibling claims cannot appear between replacement validation and route publication.
A stopping invocation, and a replacement holding its predecessor's leases, are protected only while their command refreshes them. Five minutes after the last refresh, reconciliation and claim recovery treat them like any other lease: owners verified as dead become stale, and live or ambiguous owners stay active.
