# @devfn/ports

Machine-local, lock-protected port leases with worktree instance identity, exact/preferred/range/ephemeral allocation, stale reconciliation, listener discovery, Docker mapping discovery, and generated inventory reports. Reports enumerate the complete machine registry, including other projects and profiles, and are not a current-selection view.

Selected proxy routes claim Caddy's listener ports across TCP and UDP. A normal sibling reservation, reconciliation, and garbage collection recover an expired claim only after its service leases are inactive, neither committed nor pending proxy state contains its routes, and listener inspection or a bind check confirms that the claimed TCP ports are unbound. Privileged ports require listener inspection. A verified Caddy may keep its admin listener after its last site is removed; an absent, dead, or reused owner also requires a free admin listener. Malformed route or owner state and unverified owner identity retain the claim for inspection. A healthy route or bound proxy listener keeps the ports protected while sibling worktrees start and stop.
Replacement preflight applies the same abandoned-claim recovery before checking exact ports. Registry, domain and proxy mutations share a routing lock, so sibling claims cannot appear between replacement validation and route publication.
