title: Production Readiness
description: What BillFn Phase 2 supports today and where providers still differ.
---

BillFn Phase 2 is designed to replace the legacy Nucleus Dodo and Apple billing core for the main entitlement and lifecycle flows.

## Production-ready surfaces

- lifecycle operations for cancel, change, resume, refund, sync, and restore
- normalized entitlement snapshots and quota checks
- Dodo checkout, verification, sync, refund, cancellation, change fallback, and webhooks
- Apple checkout handoff, verification, sync, restore, App Store Server Notifications v2 ingestion, notification history backfill, and Apple-managed lifecycle fallbacks
- reconciliation jobs, replayable webhook projection, and queue-driven worker execution
- TypeScript, Python, Svelte, Swift, and embedded web bridge entry points

## Provider differences that remain intentional

- Apple only performs server-side cancel and revoke when Advanced Commerce is configured and eligible.
- When Advanced Commerce is unavailable, Apple lifecycle actions return `requires_action` with `clientAction.type = "manage-subscription"`.
- Dodo change flows attempt direct provider updates first and fall back to replacement-checkout orchestration when the provider cannot mutate the subscription in place.

## Still out of scope

- Google Play and Microsoft Store production adapters
- tax, invoicing, accounting export, and seat-based enterprise billing
- internal schedulers or cron daemons; reconciliation is exposed so you can drive it with your own job runner
